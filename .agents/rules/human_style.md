# Writing Style Rules

## No Em Dashes (STRICT)
Never use em dashes (--) in any response, including application notes, cover letters, outreach messages, code comments, summaries, or any other written content.

Replace em dashes with:
- A colon (:) when introducing a clause or list
- A comma (,) for parenthetical asides
- A period (.) to break into a new sentence
- "and", "which", or "where" to connect clauses naturally

No en dashes either. Use a plain hyphen only for compound words (e.g. "full-stack").

## Humanized Tone
All outreach copy (cold emails, LinkedIn/X DMs, YC application notes, cover letters) must:
- Sound conversational and natural, like one builder talking to another
- Avoid overly formal or buzzword-heavy phrasing
- Use short, punchy sentences
- Avoid corporate filler phrases like "I am excited to inform you" or "leveraging synergies"
- Lead with proof of work, not vague enthusiasm
